# Guide 07: Hide, Delete, or Reorder a Page

**Use this when:** you want to take a page off the live site, remove it completely, or change the order that services appear in.

## Hide a page (safe and reversible)

Hiding is the safe choice. The page comes off the live site but is kept in WordPress so you can bring it back.

1. Go to **Pages** (or **Detail Pages** for a service).
2. Open the page.
3. On the right, find the **Status** setting (it says "Published").
4. Change it to **Draft**, or click **Switch to Draft**.
5. Click **Update**.

The page disappears from the live site within about a minute. To bring it back, set the Status to **Published** again and click Update.

## Delete a page (permanent)

Only do this if you are sure. Deleting cannot be easily undone.

1. Go to **Pages** (or **Detail Pages**).
2. Hover over the page name.
3. Click **Trash**.
4. The page is removed from the live site within about a minute.

If you deleted something by mistake, go to the **Trash** tab at the top of the Pages list and click **Restore**, then re-check the live site.

## Reorder services

Services (Intervention and Services sections) appear in an order you control with a number.

1. Go to **Detail Pages**.
2. Open the service you want to move.
3. Find the **Page Attributes** box (usually on the right) and the **Order** field.
4. Set the number. Smaller numbers appear first (1 is at the top, then 2, then 3, and so on).
5. Click **Update**.
6. Repeat for other services so the numbers make the order you want.

The menu and the service card grid both follow these numbers.

## When will I see it on the live site?

About 1 minute for all of the above. Refresh the live site to confirm.

## Watch out

- If you delete or hide a page that is linked from the menu, the menu link will break. Remove it from the menu too (Guide 05).
