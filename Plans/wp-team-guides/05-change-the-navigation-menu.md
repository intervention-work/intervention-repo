# Guide 05: Change the Top Navigation Menu

**Use this when:** you want to add, remove, rename, or reorder the links in the menu bar at the top of the website (Intervention, Services, Resources, and so on).

The menu on the live site is a direct mirror of the WordPress menu. Whatever you set here is what visitors see.

## Where to go

1. Log in: **https://interventiodev.wpenginepowered.com/wp-admin/**
2. In the left menu, click **Appearance**, then **Menus**.
3. Direct link: **https://interventiodev.wpenginepowered.com/wp-admin/nav-menus.php**

## Steps

### To add a link
1. On the left, choose what to add: **Pages**, **Posts**, **Custom Links**, or **Categories**.
2. Tick the box next to the item you want, then click **Add to Menu**.
3. The new item appears in the menu list on the right.
4. Click **Save Menu** (bottom right).

### To reorder links
1. In the menu list on the right, click and drag an item up or down.
2. To make an item a dropdown child, drag it slightly to the right so it nests under the item above it.
3. Click **Save Menu**.

### To rename a link
1. Click the small arrow on the right of the menu item to expand it.
2. Change the **Navigation Label**.
3. Click **Save Menu**.

### To remove a link
1. Expand the menu item (click the arrow).
2. Click **Remove**.
3. Click **Save Menu**.

## When will I see it on the live site?

About 1 minute. Refresh any page on the live site and the new menu appears everywhere (the menu is the same on every page).

## Tip

To add a brand new page (Guide 02) to the menu, first publish the page, then come here and add it using the **Pages** box on the left.
