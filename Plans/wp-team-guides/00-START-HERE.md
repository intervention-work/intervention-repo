# Start Here: How the Website Works (Read This First)

This is a 3 minute read. It explains, in plain words, how your website works now and how your edits reach the live site. The other guides show you exactly how to do each task.

## The one thing to understand

You still work in WordPress, exactly like before. Nothing about your daily work changes.

WordPress is now the "control room." The public website that visitors see is a separate, faster website. Whenever you save something in WordPress, it automatically copies over to the live website.

Think of it like a kitchen and a dining room. You cook in the WordPress kitchen. Visitors eat in the dining room (the live website). You never have to walk into the dining room. The food travels there on its own.

## What you can change from WordPress

Everything that matters for content:

- The words on any page
- Images on pages
- Blog posts
- Service pages
- The navigation menu at the top
- The phone number and email shown across the whole site

You do NOT need a developer for any of these. You do them yourself in WordPress, and they appear on the live site by themselves.

## How long until my change shows up?

- **Edited an existing page:** about 1 minute. Refresh the live page and it is there.
- **Added a brand new page:** it appears the first time someone opens its web address.
- If you do not see it after a minute, see Guide 08 (Troubleshooting). Usually you just wait a moment and refresh.

## Two golden rules

1. **Always click the blue "Update" (or "Publish") button after any change.** If you do not save in WordPress, nothing travels to the live site.
2. **Do not delete pages you are unsure about.** Hiding (unpublishing) is safer and reversible. See Guide 07.

That is the whole idea. Pick the guide for the task you want and follow the steps.
