# Guide 08: "I Don't See My Change" (Troubleshooting)

Most of the time the fix is simple. Work down this list in order.

## 1. Did you save in WordPress?

The most common cause. Every change needs the blue **Update** or **Publish** button. If you did not click it, nothing traveled to the live site. Go back and click it.

## 2. Wait one minute, then refresh

Changes take about a minute to travel. Wait 60 seconds, then refresh the live page (press F5, or the browser refresh button).

## 3. Do a "hard refresh" to beat your browser's memory

Your browser sometimes shows an old saved copy. Force it to fetch a fresh one:

- **Windows:** press **Ctrl + Shift + R**
- **Mac:** press **Cmd + Shift + R**

## 4. Check you edited the right page

Make sure the page you edited in WordPress is the same one you are viewing on the live site. Compare the web addresses. It is easy to edit one page and check another.

## 5. Is the page Published?

If the page Status is **Draft**, it will not show on the live site. Open the page in WordPress and confirm the Status says **Published** (see Guide 07).

## 6. For a brand new page: open its address directly

A new page appears the first time its web address is opened. Type the full address into your browser and press Enter. Give it a couple of seconds the first time.

## 7. New page or service not in the menu?

New pages and services do not join the menu automatically. Add them yourself (Guide 05).

## Still not showing after all this?

Then it may be a technical connection issue between WordPress and the live site. Contact the developer and tell them:

- Exactly what you changed
- The web address of the page
- The time you saved it
- That you already tried waiting a minute and a hard refresh

That information lets them fix it quickly.
